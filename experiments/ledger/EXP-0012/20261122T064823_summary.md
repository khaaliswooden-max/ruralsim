**Checks and limits**
- **Anchor:** evaporative WUE of 1.8–2.0 L per IT kWh sits at the U.S. data-center average of about 1.8 (Shehabi et al., 2016).
- **Dry cooling's cost is energy:** it uses no water but raises effective PUE on hot days (p50 1.34–1.41 versus 1.33–1.36). v1 does not feed that back into power flow.
- **Peak days are understated:** they are built from daily-mean weather, which understates hourly design peaks.
- **County use is one year old data:** 2015 only, annual.
- **Gauge statistics:** NWIS statistics are provisional for recent years.
