# EXP-0009: withdrawn (D-421, 2026-11-22)

Two runs withdrawn: they ran on Omaha and Winnebago reservation land without the nations' request.

The run files in this folder stay as they were written (the ledger is append-only, and git history is not rewritten unless a nation asks). They are not shown on ruralsim.io, are not quoted in RuralSim material, and the harness refuses to rerun this card (`experiments/WITHDRAWN.json`).

Under D-420, no experiment or screen runs on tribal land unless that nation's government requests it. The Omaha Tribal Council and the Winnebago Tribal Council are each being asked whether these results should be deleted, kept withdrawn, or handled on the nation's own terms. If there is no reply within 90 days of the letters, they stay withdrawn permanently. Either nation's answer, for its own land, replaces this note.
