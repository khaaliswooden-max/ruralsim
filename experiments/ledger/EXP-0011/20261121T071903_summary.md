Grade: **SPECULATIVE**. Simulated, screening-grade results with assumed parameters. Decision support, not a finding.

**Setup**
- Site: the county centroid (hypothetical).
- Load: 20 MW IT at a PUE of 1.1–1.5.
- Scenarios: grid only, and grid plus 30 MW solar and 60 MWh storage.
- Each scenario runs three cost allocations and every utility EIA-861 lists for the county.
- Common random numbers: both scenarios and all allocations share every draw except the on-site ones.

**Criterion, set before the run**
- No voltage and no thermal violation in at least 90% of seeds.
- p50 lowest-quintile rate change of 2% or less for every candidate utility under every allocation.
- A candidate without data is indeterminate, which is not a pass.

## Indeterminate or blocked

- **Rate criterion, Wilcox AL and Chautauqua KS (12 items):** Pioneer Electric Coop and Caney Valley El Coop file EIA's short form, so Sales_Ult_Cust has no row for them (layer request LR-10). Both are co-ops, which is the card's question.
- **Thurston NE: no site.** The county lies within the Winnebago and Omaha reservations. Tribal land is a siting constraint, so nothing is placed. Reported as blocked.
- **Lowest-quintile income share:** withheld everywhere. The SAIPE-calibrated income model puts the bottom quintile's mean income near $1,200 in Wilcox and $500 in McDowell (LR-11).
- **N-1 contingency:** not modelled.

## Passes, and what each one rests on

- **Grid.** Chautauqua passes both criteria in both scenarios; its grid-only thermal result is 10% of seeds, exactly at the bar. McDowell passes voltage in both scenarios and thermal with on-site. Wilcox with on-site passes thermal.
- **Rates.** Every candidate utility with data has a p50 rate change between −2.8% and +0.01% under every allocation. **These passes rest on one assumed input.** For every utility, county, scenario and allocation, the rank correlation across seeds between the rate result and `margin_on_new_sales_share` is between −0.83 and −0.99. That input is the margin the utility keeps on the site's energy sales, drawn from −10% to +25% of site revenue. No public source pins it per utility, so every rate pass is conditional on it.
- **Utility size sets the scale.** For Alabama Power (1.3 M residential customers), Evergy Kansas South (300 k) and Appalachian Power (350 k), the site moves rates by hundredths of a percent. For Black Warrior EMC and Clarke-Washington EMC (about 20 k each), the p10–p90 band runs from −7.6% to +2.6%.

## Against EXP-0007 v0

v0 put Wilcox at −23.8% (grid only) and +16.7% (with on-site), on fixed inputs and with no load flow. Its 20 seeds were identical.

In v1, for the two co-ops with data, the p50 is −2.8% to −1.6% grid-only and −2.1% to −1.3% with on-site. v0's on-site increase came from charging the solar and storage capex to ratepayers. v1 treats those as the site's own asset and records their cost separately.
