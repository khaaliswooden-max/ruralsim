## What changed (D-102)

1. **Short-form co-ops now have data.** Pioneer Electric Coop (Wilcox) and Caney Valley El Coop (Chautauqua) report only all-sector totals (EIA-861S, LR-10). Their rate base, average rate and kWh per customer are all-sector stand-ins for residential figures. Every record marks them `rate_basis: all_sectors_stand_in`.
2. **The income effect is now reported.** The lowest-quintile bill change is divided by ACS 2020–2024 mean income of the lowest quintile, drawn inside its margin of error (Wilcox $7,714 ± 1,566; McDowell $6,960 ± 1,594; LR-11).
3. **New validity limit on the rate model.** When the site's annual grid energy exceeds 25% of a utility's own sales, the rate criterion is indeterminate. The computed values are kept, labelled, and not scored. The model treats the site as one customer among many. Once the site would be the utility's largest load by far, the outcome turns on a large-load contract (often signed by the G&T, not the co-op), and no public source gives that.
