## 5. What G1's PLAUSIBLE does and does not mean

- **The test could not fail in the direction it was run.** `power_flow.run` adds `{"type": "substation", "subtype": "new customer substation"}` to `required_upgrades` for every computed seed. `share_seeds_new_substation` is 1.0 whenever the power flow computes. Every site had a documented substation, so every site passes on both bounds.
- **This was knowable before the run and was not caught.** D-208 said v1's G1 pass "would carry no information" because the engine called a substation in 100% of seeds. D-210 mapped G1 to the same field without checking whether the engine can return "no". That is a run-plan error. The grade stands because §8 freezes the method after the first run.
- **What would make G1 informative (v3):** a G1 output the engine can answer both ways, such as an expansion versus new-build distinction or a transmission upgrade beyond the customer tap, and held-out sites where no build was needed.
