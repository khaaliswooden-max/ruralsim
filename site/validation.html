Every world, checked against the record.

Each county RuralSim builds is checked against public data before it is published: county area against the Census, terrain coverage, bridges against the federal bridge inventory, population history, buildings against housing units, and water systems placed. Flags are shown here with the same prominence as passes.

A flag means a world differs from its reference dataset by more than the check allows, or a record could not be placed. It is a reason to look, not a verdict on the county. Each report lists every check, the numbers on both sides, and the data sources. Pilot counties are checked from the reports in the repository; every other county from the snapshot it was published with.

RuralSim is a free, open-source project by Aldrich K. Wooden, Sr., founder of Get Rural America Done. Simulated, screening-grade results with assumed parameters. Decision support, not a finding.
