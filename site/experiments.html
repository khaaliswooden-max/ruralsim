Counties Experiments Every county Company Source

Ten ideas, tested before anyone pays for them.

Each idea ran 20 times on a different set of 2,000 simulated households in one pilot county. The success line for each was written down before it ran. An idea clears the bar only if the middle 90% of its runs lands past that line and none of the six protections for residents is broken.

Screening grade: simplified engines, assumed equipment numbers. Nothing here is a finding until confirmed with full-physics models.
